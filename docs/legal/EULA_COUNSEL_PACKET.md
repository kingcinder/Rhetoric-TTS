## Product terms reflected in the draft

- One-time purchase of a serial number; no subscription.
- One product is offered under a one-time serial number; there is no subscription.
- Without a valid serial number the application may be viewed, but functional
  features are unavailable.
- Offline activation and no telemetry.
- All sales final, subject to any rights that applicable law requires.
- Proposed defaults, clearly marked for review: one named licensee using the
  licensee's own machines, no machine binding, current-major-version scope,
  and non-transferability.
- Washington governing law and venue retained as draft terms for review.
- Electronic acceptance records the accepted text/version and is intended to
  operate as an electronic contract only where the parties agree to transact
  electronically.
- The draft includes savings clauses for non-waivable consumer remedies,
  mandatory warranties, refund rights, venue rules, and open-source rights.
- The draft includes proposed voice-consent, synthetic-audio disclosure,
  watermark, export, indemnity, and liability language; each remains subject
  to counsel review.

## Washington-law references for counsel verification

These are research references, not legal conclusions. Counsel should verify the
current law and its application to the actual seller, transaction, and customer
locations:

- Washington Uniform Electronic Transactions Act, RCW 1.80, including
  electronic-contract recognition, agreement to transact electronically, and
  attribution of electronic signatures.
- Washington Consumer Protection Act, chapter 19.86 RCW.
- Washington sales-tax and prewritten-software provisions, including RCW
  82.04.215 and RCW 82.12.020, as applicable to the sales channel.
- Washington commercial warranty and remedy provisions, including RCW
  62A.2-316 and RCW 62A.2-719, to confirm conspicuousness and non-waivable
  limits.
