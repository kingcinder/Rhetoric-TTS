# EULA Washington-Law Validation — Rhetoric 1.5.0

- **Validation date:** 2026-11-08
- **Document validated:** `EULA.md` (finalized from `EULA-DRAFT.md` by owner order, 2026-11-08)
- **Method:** each material clause was checked against primary sources in the WA Law Corpus on this machine at `/media/cody/Data/DOCUMENTS_PROJECT_FILES/WA LAW ALL FILES` (title/chapter files under `rcw/`). Citations below are to sections whose text was read in the corpus during this validation. Anything not read in the corpus is labeled **unverified**.
- **Standing caveat:** this is an owner-side, corpus-based validation, not a legal opinion. Independent review by qualified counsel has **not** been obtained and is not claimed anywhere in the EULA or this memo. Counsel review remains the one gate only a human attorney can close.
