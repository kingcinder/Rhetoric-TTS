# Product Offer Assessment — Owner and Counsel Review Required

## Product model

The 1.5.0 release candidate is a local-first desktop application and CLI
licensed through a **one-time serial number**. There is no subscription,
Community edition, trial edition, or optional activation path. Without a valid
serial number the application may open for activation, license status, help,
and quitting, but program features remain inert. With a valid serial number,
the complete supported feature set is available within the licensed version
scope.

The proposed default terms are one named licensee using the software on that
licensee's own machines, with no machine binding, and coverage for releases
in the current major version. These are proposals pending owner and counsel
approval.

## Distribution and qualification status

The candidate is not approved for commercial distribution. Linux x86_64 is the
intended qualification target. Windows is unsupported and excluded from the
proposed paid offer until its GUI and installer qualification passes. CUDA and
Vulkan are experimental. Frozen artifacts, clean-machine installation,
offline activation, model download/resume, rollback, uninstall, and human audio
review remain open unless `FINAL_RELEASE_READINESS.md` records direct evidence.

The EULA remains a draft. Pricing, payment processing, support commitments,
refund language, permitted voice use, export controls, and third-party license
decisions require owner and counsel approval. The current proposal is all
sales final subject to mandatory law and payment-processor requirements.

## Owner and counsel decisions

1. Approve the one-time serial-number model and proposed license scope.
2. Approve the proposed no-refund language after payment-processor and
   jurisdiction review.
3. Approve voice-consent, impersonation, export, warranty, liability, and
   indemnity language in the EULA draft.
4. Approve third-party license decisions and the distribution treatment of
   LGPL components.
5. Approve qualification claims only after the linked hosted and installed-
   artifact evidence is complete.
